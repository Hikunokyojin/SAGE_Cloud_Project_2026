What each outcome means
serve
The catalogue has the kind of service asked for, and the stated limits look achievable. Pick the service kind (or kinds, in order, for a chain).
review
It asks for real kinds of service, but no option could meet the limits (impossible budget, 100% uptime, contradictory limits). A human should look at it. Pick the kinds too.
out of catalogue
It asks for something none of the listed service kinds covers.
ambiguous
Too vague to act on, or readable more than one way. The system should ask a question back.
block
An attack or misuse: trying to override the system's rules, steal secrets, or do something harmful or illegal. Refuse outright.

If a request mixes a harmful aim with a normal one, choose block. Use the notes box when you are unsure; it helps when we compare answers.
